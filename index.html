About

I am an AI researcher and engineer with over six years of industry experience in artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on generative AI and quantum machine learning.

My industry work includes developing large-scale retrieval-augmented generation and agentic AI systems, as well as leading quantum machine learning and optimisation projects.

Research

My research focuses on frugal and uncertainty-aware machine learning, particularly in settings where obtaining data, labels, or computational feedback is expensive. I develop methods for cost-aware active learning, algorithm selection, uncertainty quantification, and statistical machine learning, with applications to combinatorial optimisation and AI-based decision making.

I am also interested in quantum machine learning, including quantum optimisation and resource-efficient training of quantum machine learning models.
