Erdem Kus

AI Researcher and Engineer

I am a Research Fellow at the University of St Andrews, developing a GenAI-based recommendation system for the AI for Academic Synergy project. I recently completed my PhD in Computer Science there.

My research focuses on frugal and cost-aware machine learning, including active learning, algorithm selection, uncertainty quantification, and AI for decision making.

I also have 6 years of industry experience at Nokia, building large-scale Generative AI, RAG, multi-agent systems, and quantum machine learning and optimisation systems.

For research collaborations, industry projects, or consultancy, please feel free to get in touch: kuserdem16@gmail.com
